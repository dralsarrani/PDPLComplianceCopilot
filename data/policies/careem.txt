2. Legal Basis for the Processing of Personal Data
Depending on applicable privacy laws, where you use our Services, and our purpose for using your data, Uber relies on the following legal basis for processing your data:
Contractual Necessity: This basis applies when we must use your data in order to provide the services you request and meet our responsibilities under the contract to which you are a party to. For example, to provide and maintain our Services and to send administrative or Service-related messages.
Consent: This legal basis applies when we notify you how we will collect and use your data and you voluntarily agree to that use of your data. Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, in which case we will cease that collection and use of your data. For example, to send personalized marketing communications.
Legitimate Interest: This legal basis applies when Uber or a third party to whom personal data has been disclosed processes your data for legitimate purposes (such as for purposes of safety, security, and fraud detection and prevention), provided this processing of data is necessary for those purposes and the benefits of those purposes are not in conflict with your fundamental rights and freedoms. For example, we process personal data to analyze and enhance the performance of our services, based on our legitimate interest in improving service quality and ensuring the platform operates efficiently for all.
Legal Obligation: This legal basis applies when we are required to comply with a mandatory legal requirement or order issued by a competent court. For example, to screen Captains and Riders before enabling certain services.
