To deliver your orders to the correct address.

Jarir Cookie Consent Policy
Introduction
At Jarir, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. This Cookie Consent Policy outlines our practices concerning the use of cookies on our website, in accordance with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other relevant regulations.
What is Cookie Consent?
Cookie consent is the permission granted by users to allow a website to store or retrieve information on their device, primarily in the form of cookies. This information helps us understand user preferences, improve their browsing experience, and tailor our services accordingly.
Types of Cookies Used
We use various types of cookies on our website, categorized as follows:
Mandatory Cookies: Essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They do not store any personally identifiable information.
Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests.
Performance Cookies: Allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site.
Social Cookies: Enable you to share our content with your friends and networks.
Functional Cookies: Enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization.
Location Data
We may request access to your location data (precise or approximate) through your mobile device or browser settings. This information is used solely to enhance your experience and enable certain features, such as providing location-based content, nearest store details, or other relevant services.
Collection of Location Data:
We collect location data through technologies such as GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular network signals.
Your device may provide prompts or notifications when we request access to your location. You can choose to accept or decline these requests.
Purpose of Collecting Location Data:
Service Delivery: We use your location data to deliver services tailored to your current location, such as the nearest branch information or location-based promotions.
Enhancements & Personalization: Location data helps us personalize your experience, ensuring that you receive the most relevant offers, updates, and recommendations.
Consent Management
Upon your first visit to our website, you will be prompted to provide your consent for the use of cookies, except for mandatory cookies, which do not require consent. You can customize your preferences through our cookie settings page at any time.
Revoking or Updating Consent
Should you decide to revoke or update your consent, we will respect your choices and remove any cookies that are not categorized as mandatory. Our website provides easy access for you to manage your cookie preferences.
'Cookie Policy' Cookie
The 'cookie Policy' cookie is used to store your consent preferences. It includes the domain, consent duration, and consent values, which represent your choices regarding the different categories of cookies.
Privacy Statement
In addition to this Cookie Consent Policy, our use of cookie and consent management requires a separate acknowledgment of the Jarir privacy statement to ensure full transparency and compliance.
